# muse-pigeon1126f-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf).

## The entry

> Forget drones: my flock is 10,000 couriers who already work for breadcrumbs, know every rooftop shortcut in the city, and have never once asked for parking — fund Coo-rier and we deliver by lunch.

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it is unmistakably a pitch to investors (fund us, we deliver), its couriers could only be pigeons (breadcrumbs, rooftop shortcuts, no parking), and the drone comparison gives it a real startup logic under the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
